What the light means

    To change the Wi-Fi network later, hold the BOOT button on the camera for 3 seconds while it’s running. The light flashes yellow, then the camera waits for this page. Unplugging it or pressing RST keeps its settings. You can turn the light off on the camera’s live view page.
